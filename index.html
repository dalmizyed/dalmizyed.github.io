About

I’m a sophomore at the University of Wisconsin–Madison earning a B.S. in Mechanical Engineering and a Certificate in Computer Sciences (May 2029). I design, build, and test mechanical systems, and I’m working toward a career in the space industry.

I’m Mechanisms Lead for Wisconsin Autonomous Underwater Vehicles (WAUV). I lead 15+ engineers through the design, manufacturing, integration, and testing of the mechanisms on our autonomous submarine, including the gripper, torpedo launchers, and tag droppers. I modeled and prototyped a tendon-actuated gripper that lifts hand-sized objects up to 6 kg, and I develop the test procedures that check each mechanism against competition requirements. We’re debuting as UW–Madison’s first competitive marine robotics team at RoboSub 2027.

I’m also a Design Engineer on Wisconsin Aerospace (SAE Aero UW). I own the design, optimization, and manufacture of the aircraft’s structural members, and I’m validating designs with compressive load-frame testing and in-flight strain gage data, then using the results to guide further design of the aircraft structure.

I’m interested in the design and propulsion of rockets and aircraft, and in robots built for tasks humans can’t easily do, such as space travel and underwater exploration.

I spent summer 2026 as a mechanical engineering intern at AprilAire, and I’m now seeking a summer 2027 internship in the aerospace industry where I can apply my design, testing, and analysis skills.
